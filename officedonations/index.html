Donate Office Equipment

Have computers, monitors, or other tech gathering dust? We will put them to work for Kansas City youth. Fill out the form below and we will be in touch.

What We Accept

Equipment That Makes a Difference

We accept used office computers, monitors, keyboards, mice, and other peripherals in working or repairable condition. Every piece of equipment you donate gets refurbished and placed with a Kansas City youth who needs it. Nothing goes to waste.

Desktop & Laptop Computers

Office desktops and laptops of any age. We test and refurbish everything.

Monitors

Any size or resolution. Even older monitors are welcome.

Peripherals & Other Tech

Keyboards, mice, webcams, headsets, cables, and more.
