Olivia Gonsher Founder & CEO
Meet the Founder

Hi! I'm Olivia

I'm a high school student from Kansas City who loves gaming and technology. I started building PCs for fun, and it made me realize how powerful access to technology can be.

That's why I created Connections KC, to build and donate custom PCs to kids who might not have the same opportunities. My goal is to give them a chance to explore gaming, learn STEM skills, and connect with their community, just like I have.

Our Mission

Empowering Young People Through Technology

Connections KC builds and donates custom PCs to kids in the Kansas City area, giving them access to technology, gaming, and STEM opportunities. Our goal is to empower young people to learn, create, and connect, providing tools they might not otherwise have.
