Meet the Founder

Hi! I'm Olivia

I'm a 15-year-old student at Pembroke Hill School in Kansas City who loves gaming and technology. I started building computers for fun, and it made me realize how powerful access to technology can be.

So I started Connections KC. We refurbish computers and get them into the hands of kids who otherwise wouldn't have one. I want every kid to have the chance to discover what technology can do for them.

Our Mission

Empowering Young People Through Technology

Mission Statement

Connections KC refurbishes and donates computers to kids in the Kansas City area, giving them access to technology, gaming, and STEM opportunities. Our goal is to empower young people to learn, create, and connect, providing tools they might not otherwise have.

Gaming

A computer opens up an entirely new world of games, stories, and creativity that many kids never get to experience.

STEM Skills

Building and using computers teaches problem-solving and technical skills that matter well beyond the classroom.

Community

Technology brings people together. We want every kid in Kansas City to have a seat at that table.
