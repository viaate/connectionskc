What You Will Receive

A Real Computer, Ready to Use

The computers we donate are fully tested and ready to use. They can run popular games like Fortnite, Minecraft, Valorant, and Roblox smoothly, and handle schoolwork, browsing, and creative projects without any issues. They are not brand-new high-end machines and won't max out the latest releases, but they are solid, reliable computers that will give you a real gaming and computing experience. Think of it like a well-maintained used car rather than a new one off the lot.
